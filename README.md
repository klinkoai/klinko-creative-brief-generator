## What is Klinko Creative Brief Generator?

Klinko Creative Brief Generator is an AI agent Skill for translating audience and market context into clear creative direction. It organizes the objective, target audience, customer tension, desired response, message hierarchy, supporting points, tone, deliverables, and constraints so internal teams and external creators can produce work from the same strategic foundation.

## What this AI agent Skill helps you do

- Turn research and strategy into a usable creative brief
- Define the audience, objective, and central customer tension
- Create a focused message hierarchy and creative direction
- Align stakeholders before copy, design, or production begins

## Questions you can ask

- “Create a creative brief for this campaign.”
- “Turn this audience insight into an ad brief.”
- “What should our creative team communicate to this persona?”
- “Build a launch brief for multiple content formats.”

## What you receive

| Deliverable | Decision it supports |
|---|---|
| Creative objective | Clarify the intended business and audience response |
| Audience and tension | Ground the work in a specific customer context |
| Message hierarchy | Keep communication focused |
| Direction and constraints | Give creators actionable boundaries |

## Example workflow

1. Describe the product, idea, audience, or market you want to understand.
2. Add relevant public context such as geography, category, goals, or constraints.
3. Ask a clear decision question rather than requesting generic research.
4. Review the structured answer and use follow-up questions to refine the decision.

### Example prompt

> Create a creative brief for a launch campaign promoting an AI meeting assistant to remote product teams. The campaign should drive free-trial signups.

### Example result structure

- A precise objective and audience definition
- The customer tension the creative should dramatize
- Primary message, supporting points, and desired response
- Tone, formats, mandatories, and creative guardrails
